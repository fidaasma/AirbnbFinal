About this place

Experience Luxury & Comfort at our Stylish 2BHK apartment Near Manyata & Hebbal Welcome to our thoughtfully designed luxury 2BHK apartment, where premium comfort meets modern aesthetics. Whether you're in Bangalore for work, a family vacation, or a long-term stay, our home is designed to make you feel at ease the moment you step in. We take pride in offering a hotel-like experience with the warmth of home. Our fully furnished apartment features plush seating, a fully equipped kitchen, and serene bedrooms designed for restful sleep. Located just minutes away from Manyata Tech Park and Hebbal, this apartment is ideal for business travellers and families alike, with easy access to restaurants, cafes and the outer ring road. The living room opens onto a private balcony with garden views, perfect for your morning coffee. Both bedrooms come with attached bathrooms, plush bedding and blackout curtains for a peaceful night's sleep. Guests also get access to building amenities including a lift, 24/7 security and dedicated parking. Whether you're here for business or leisure, our home is designed to make your stay memorable.
